📋 How to get a referral code
  1. Log in to your Rent Nivas account on any device.
  2. Look at the left sidebar — just below your email address you will see your personal Ref. Code displayed (e.g. RC: 12345678).
  3. Click the code to instantly copy it to your clipboard.

⚙️ Platform Settings

Control how credits flow between renters and owners.

🔓 Unlock Owner Reward Rate

When Person B unlocks Person A's listing using 1 credit, how many credits does Person A (the owner) receive?
Default is 1. Set to 0.8 means platform keeps 0.2, owner gets 0.8.

Examples:
1 → owner gets 1 full credit per unlock
0.8 → owner gets 0.8 credits per unlock
0 → owner gets nothing (platform keeps all)
